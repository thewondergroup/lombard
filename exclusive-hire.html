Exclusive hire

The whole building, just for you

Enquire hereEvents brochure

Hire 1 Lombard Street exclusively and every room is yours: the Brasserie under its glass cupola, our private dining room 1776 and The Lombard Club downstairs. A Grade II listed former banking hall opposite Bank station, and a restaurant since 1998, run by an events team with an eye for every detail.

The space

Beneath the glass cupola

The Brasserie is the centre of it all: a former banking hall beneath an Italian-designed glass cupola, with bespoke architecture and curated artworks all around. It can be set up three ways.

Exclusive hire also includes 1776 (up to 80 standing) and The Lombard Club (up to 40 standing). Use them for a welcome drinks reception, a breakout space or a late-night bar.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What is the capacity for exclusive hire?

Up to 150 guests fully seated, up to 100 seated with a dance floor, or up to 300 standing. 1776 (up to 80 standing) and The Lombard Club (up to 40 standing) are included.

What times can the venue be hired?

Exclusive hire is available for breakfast (08:00 to 10:30), lunch (12:00 to 15:00) or an evening event (18:00 to midnight).

How is exclusive hire priced?

Pricing is based on a minimum spend and room hire, depending on the date and time of day. The events team will put together a quote.

Something smaller?

Our private dining rooms seat from 12 to 50, with their own bars.
