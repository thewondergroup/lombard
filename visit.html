Good to know

Questions, answered

Where is 1 Lombard Street?

1 Lombard Street, London EC3V 9AA, in the City of London, directly opposite Bank station on the Central, Northern, Waterloo & City lines and the DLR.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday 7am to 11pm, Saturday bottomless brunch 12pm to 5.30pm, and Sunday lunch 12pm to 6pm.

How do I book a table?

Book online through SevenRooms or call 020 7929 6611. For events and private dining, email events@1lombardstreet.com.

What is the nearest station?

Bank station is directly opposite. Monument is a short walk and Cannon Street is around five minutes away.
