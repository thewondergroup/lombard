Good to know

Questions, answered

How many wedding guests can 1 Lombard Street hold?

With exclusive hire, up to 150 seated for the wedding breakfast, up to 100 seated with a dance floor, or up to 300 for a standing reception. For a smaller wedding, 1776 seats up to 50, or 24 on one long table, and holds up to 80 standing.

How late can a wedding run?

Celebrations with exclusive hire can run until midnight.

Can we visit before booking?

Yes. The events team will invite you both in for a show-round. Call 020 7929 6611 or email events@1lombardstreet.com.
