# 1 Lombard Street — website redesign (test site)

Static redesign of the 1 Lombard Street website, by The Wonder Agency.
It is hosted on GitHub Pages for review only and set to `noindex`, so search engines ignore it. The live Wix site is untouched.

## Pages
- `index.html`: homepage, with the animated producers map
- `menus.html`: every menu on one page with sticky tabs (breakfast, à la carte, vegetarian, desserts, brunch, Sunday lunch, drinks)
- `whats-on.html`: events and offers
- `fridays.html`, `bottomless-brunch.html`, `sunday-lunch.html`, `christmas.html`: individual experiences
- `private-dining.html`, `exclusive-hire.html`, `weddings.html`: events pages from the client's copy, with capacity tables and enquiry forms; forms open an email to the events team until the CRM or DesignMyNight link is added
- `our-story.html`: the history, Head Chef and the scrolling producers map journey
- `gift-cards.html`, `loyalty.html`
- `visit.html`: directions, hours, contact and careers
- `404.html`

## Shared files
- `assets/site.css`, `assets/site.js`, `assets/img/`: shared styles, script, logos and favicon
- `images/`: producers map artwork and animation sprites
- `images/photos/`: the venue's own photography, resized for the web (`-l` 2000px, `-m` 1000px)

## Older versions, kept for reference
- `v1.html`: the first design
- `producers-map.html`: the animated map on its own
- `producers-journey.html`: the scrolling map journey on its own

## Before launch
Most photos are now served from `images/photos/`. A few still load from the live Wix site: the hero video and its poster, the Christmas dining room, Head Chef James Holden, Goosebox, and the wedding and Fridays galleries. Download those into `images/` too.

## SEO and GEO
- Every page has its own title and description, a canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter share tags, and local geo tags.
- Structured data (JSON-LD) on every page: Restaurant (address, coordinates, hours, phone, booking action, cuisine, founder, head chef), WebSite, WebPage and breadcrumbs. Page extras: the full Menu with sections, items and prices (menus); weekly Events (brunch, Fridays, Sunday lunch); the Christmas event; rooms and capacities (private dining); EventVenue (exclusive hire, weddings); People (our story).
- Visible FAQ sections, also marked up as FAQPage, on menus, Fridays, brunch, Sunday lunch, private dining, exclusive hire, weddings and visit, written as short factual answers that AI search tools can quote.
- `llms.txt`: a plain-text summary of the key facts and pages for AI assistants. `sitemap.xml` lists every page.

### At launch
1. Remove the `noindex` tag and replace `robots.txt` with one that allows crawling and points to the sitemap. Both are generated by the build.
2. Check that the canonical URLs match the final domain (currently https://www.1lombardstreet.com/).
3. Set up 301 redirects from the old Wix URLs (for example `/fridays`, `/weddings`, `/bottomless-brunch`, `/whats-on`) to the new pages, so existing Google rankings carry over.
4. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and make sure the Google Business Profile hours and links match the site.
