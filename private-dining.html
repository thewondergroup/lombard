Private dining

Private dining in the heart of the City

Enquire hereEvents brochure

Since 1998, in a Grade II listed former banking hall opposite Bank station, we have two private dining rooms and a semi-private corner of our Brasserie. Whether it's a board dinner for 12 or a launch for 80, you get a room to yourselves, a team that knows how to look after a table, and food from our own kitchen.

Our rooms

Three spaces, one kitchen

Private dining room

1776

Tucked just behind the Brasserie, flooded with natural daylight and overlooking Mansion House Place. It has its own private bar and a built-in TV, so it works as well for a launch as it does for a long dinner.

Food and drink

From our own kitchen

Everything comes from our own kitchen, with seasonal British set menus and canapé packages for drinks receptions. Our wine list and cocktail bar take care of the rest, and drinks packages can be agreed in advance so there are no surprises on the bill.

Tell us about dietary requirements when you book and we'll plan around every guest. We also have vegetarian and vegan menus, and seasonal events packages. Download the events brochure for full menus.
