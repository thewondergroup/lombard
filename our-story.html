Our story

The City's dining room

A Grade II listed banking hall at Bank, and a kitchen built on British produce.

The building

Once a banking hall

1 Lombard Street sits at the very heart of the Square Mile, on the corner of Bank junction. The dining room is a Grade II listed former banking hall, crowned by a glass cupola that fills the room with light.

Since 1998 the City has gathered here for breakfast meetings, long lunches and late dinners, from deal-closing lunches to wedding parties.

The kitchen

Head Chef James Holden

James's menus celebrate the seasons: fish from British shores, fruit and vegetables from local growers, and meat from small, award-winning producers.

Dishes are simply served and honestly sourced, from Peter Hannan's salt-aged beef to Clarence Court eggs and Neal's Yard cheeses.

“We focus on the quality and backstory of our ingredients just as much as how we cook them.”
